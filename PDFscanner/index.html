Good to know

Questions, answered.

What can I scan with PDF Scanner?

Receipts, contracts, forms, handwritten notes, book pages, IDs, business cards, and most other flat paper documents.

Can I combine multiple pages?

Yes. Capture pages one after another, reorder them, and export everything as a single PDF.

Does it recognize text?

PDF Scanner includes OCR tools designed to identify printed text so you can copy and reuse it instead of typing it again.

Can I edit a scan before exporting?

Yes. You can refine the crop, rotate pages, apply enhancement filters, reorder pages, and prepare the document before export.
